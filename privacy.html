Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 26, 2025

Overview

Kombat X ("the App") is a ballistic calculator designed for long-range shooters. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Collection

Kombat X does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

All data you enter in the App — including rifles, ammunition, ballistic calculations, and settings — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage. None of this data ever leaves your device or is sent to any server.

Location Data

The App may request access to your device's location. This data is used solely to:

  • Fetch local weather conditions (temperature, pressure, humidity) via Apple WeatherKit for atmospheric modeling in ballistic calculations.
  • Provide compass heading for wind direction reference.

Your location data is sent directly to Apple's WeatherKit service to retrieve weather information. It is not collected, stored, or transmitted by us. You can deny location access at any time in your device settings; the App will continue to function with manually entered atmospheric data.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following Apple service:

  • Apple WeatherKit — to retrieve local weather conditions based on your device location. Apple's own privacy policy governs how WeatherKit handles location data.

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any user data with third parties. There is no user data to share — everything stays on your device.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the Support page.
