4-DOF Ballistic Solver

Go beyond standard point-mass calculations. The 4-DOF engine uses RK4 integration with the G7 drag model, adding spin drift (gyroscopic), Coriolis effect, and Eötvös correction — with a full decomposition of each contribution so you know exactly what's affecting your shot.

Point Blank Range Calculator

Instantly compute your maximum point blank range, near zero, far zero, and vertex for any rifle and ammo combination. Includes sight-in parameters at 100 yards — dial in your setup before heading to the field.

3-DOF Trajectory

Classic point-mass solver with G1 and G7 drag models. Interactive trajectory charts and ballistic tables with drop, windage, velocity, energy, and scope corrections at every range.
