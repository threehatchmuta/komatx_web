Frequently Asked Questions

What drag models are supported?

Kombat X supports both G1 and G7 drag models, the industry-standard models used by professional ballistic calculators.

How does weather integration work?

The app uses Apple WeatherKit to fetch local temperature, pressure, and humidity based on your current location. You can also enter atmospheric conditions manually.

What units are supported?

Full metric and imperial support. Distances in meters or yards, velocities in m/s or ft/s, mass in grams or grains, and scope adjustments in MOA or MRAD.

Is my data stored on a server?

No. All your data — rifles, ammunition, calculations, and settings — is stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All ballistic calculations work fully offline. The only feature requiring internet is automatic weather fetching — you can always enter atmospheric data manually.
